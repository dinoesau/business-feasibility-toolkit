# 04 - Glossary

> **Legal validity: Mexico only.**

## Market and strategy

- **TAM / SAM / SOM:** total, serviceable, obtainable market. SOM feeds the sales forecast.
- **Value proposition:** why customers choose you over competitors.
- **SWOT:** strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats (2x2 matrix in the idea sheet).
- **MVP:** minimum viable product to validate with real users.
- **CAC / LTV / churn:** acquisition cost, customer lifetime value, abandonment rate.
- **Go-to-market:** channels, alliances, marketing, launch timeline.

## Finance

- **NPV:** net present value; > 0 creates value at the chosen discount rate.
- **IRR:** internal rate of return; compare against cost of capital.
- **Payback:** investment recovery period.
- **Breakeven:** units or revenue where profit = 0.
- **EBITDA / cash flow:** operating profit before interest/tax/depreciation; actual cash generated.
- **Sensitivity:** how NPV/IRR move when price, cost, or volume vary.

## MX legal (detail in `05-legal-framework-MX.md`)

- **SAS / SRL / SA de C.V.:** entity types (comparison in doc 05).
- **RFC / e.firma:** SAT tax ID and electronic signature.
- **IMSS / INFONAVIT:** social security and housing; employer registration (Registro Patronal) mandatory with payroll.
- **Land use / operating license:** municipal permits.
- **NOM:** Norma Oficial Mexicana; certification by accredited lab.
- **IMPI:** trademarks and patents office.
- **REPSE:** STPS registry for specialized services (outsourcing).
- **Vesting / cliff:** progressive share release (standard 4 years + 12-month cliff).
- **Drag-along / tag-along:** majority squeeze-out and minority co-sale rights.
- **Cap table:** capitalization table (who owns what %).
- **LOI / term sheet:** letter of intent / investment terms.
- **LGSM, CFF, LSS, LFMN, LFPI, LFT:** applicable Mexican laws.

## Methodology

- **Pre-feasibility:** quick filter before the full study.
- **Feasibility:** proof through 6 studies.
- **Go/No-Go:** documented decision to execute, adjust, or discard.
- **Due diligence:** document verification before investing or buying.
- **SCIAN:** MX activity classifier (equivalent of Colombia's CIIU cited in base guides).
