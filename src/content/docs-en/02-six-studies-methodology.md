# 02 - Six-studies methodology

> **Legal validity: Mexico only.**

Final document structure: executive summary, introduction (objective, scope, methodology, sources), 6 studies, integral evaluation, annexes.

## Study 1 - Market

- Current and projected demand, trends, demographic and psychographic segmentation.
- Competition: count, shares, strengths/weaknesses, entry barriers.
- Price and positioning: ranges, sensitivity, perceived value.
- Channels and logistics: routes, costs, alliances.
- Sales forecast in units and value, 3-5 years.
- MX sources: INEGI, BANXICO, Data Mexico, own surveys.
- Output: the sales table that feeds the financial study. Format: narrative + tables + charts.

## Study 2 - Technical

- Production process as flowchart, technologies, installed capacity.
- Location and infrastructure: access, utilities, layout.
- Equipment: list with specs and cost (buy or lease).
- Operations plan: shifts, labor, critical inputs.
- Gantt schedule: build, testing, go-live.

## Study 3 - Organizational

- Org chart with roles and responsibilities.
- Profiles: technical and management skills, training plan.
- HR policies: hiring, salaries, incentives.
- Governance: steering committee, risk matrix (optional).

## Study 4 - Legal (summary; detail in `05-legal-framework-MX.md`)

- Entity type (SAS, SRL, SA de C.V.), RFC and e.firma.
- Permits: land use, operating license, sectoral permits.
- IP: IMPI trademarks/patents.
- Key contracts and legal risks.
- Format: requirements table with done / pending / N/A status.

## Study 5 - Financial

- Itemized initial investment; operating costs (materials, payroll, utilities, maintenance).
- Revenue from the market study (same figure in plan, summary, and deck).
- Projected statements 3-5 years: P&L, balance sheet, cash flow.
- Indicators: NPV, IRR, payback, breakeven.
- Sensitivity to price, cost, volume; funding sources (equity, debt, NAFIN/funds).
- Format: tables + cash-flow chart + sensitivity tornado.

## Study 6 - Environmental

- Baseline (soil, water, air, biodiversity).
- Aspect-impact matrix (positive and negative).
- Applicable law (LGEEPA, EIA, environmental NOMs) and permits.
- Mitigation and monitoring plan with costs.
- Environmental viability conclusion.

## Integral evaluation

- Go/No-Go table per study (green/yellow/red) + recommendation: execute, adjust, or discard.
- Cross-cutting risks and contingency plan; optional: Canvas and triple bottom line (ESG).
