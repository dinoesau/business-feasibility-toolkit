# 05 - Legal framework MX

> **Disclaimer: this is not legal advice.** Educational content valid for Mexico only. Laws, fees, and timelines change; verify in the DOF and with a notary, accountant, or lawyer before acting. The Spanish version is authoritative for legal terms.

## 1. Choose an entity (LGSM)

| Entity | Partners | Capital | Governance | Best for | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SAS** (Simplified Stock Company) | 1+ | No legal minimum | Single director, 100% online, no statutory auditor | Solo founder, fast cheap start | Poor for preferred shares / institutional investors |
| **S. de R.L. de C.V.** | 2-50 | Practical reference $3,000 MXN | Single manager possible, no auditor | Family, JV, controlled subsidiary | Quota transfers need partner consent |
| **S.A. de C.V.** | Min. 2 | No legal minimum | Board or single director + **mandatory comisario** | Raising equity, stock options, corporate image | Higher admin burden and cost |

Path: name reservation (Secretary of Economy) -> articles of incorporation -> notarial deed -> Public Registry of Commerce (1-2 wks) -> RFC.

## 2. RFC and e.firma (SAT)

1. CURP (Civil Registry/consulate for foreigners).
2. Pre-registration at `citas.sat.gob.mx`.
3. In-person appointment: ID, printed CURP, MX fiscal address proof, email. Typical wait 1-4 weeks.
4. Constancia de Situacion Fiscal same day + e.firma (+20-30 min). Government fee: free.
5. Without an active RFC, IMSS, banking, and REPSE are blocked.

## 3. IMSS and INFONAVIT (mandatory with payroll)

1. Employer registration (Registro Patronal) on the IMSS portal with active RFC and fiscal address.
2. Register each worker within 5 business days (CURP, NSS, salary base, contract; immigration status if foreign).
3. Employer contributions approx. 20-30% of salary, monthly; monthly reports and salary updates.
4. Typical time 1-2 weeks with documents ready.

## 4. Municipal licenses and land use

1. Check zoning on the municipal urban viewer (e.g. Visor Urbano in Jalisco).
2. Land-use license: ownership or lease, plans, RFC, representative ID.
3. Operating license: same plus activity description and sectoral permits.
4. Typical fee $1,000-10,000 MXN by activity/municipality; 3 days on digital platforms, 2-4 weeks on paper; possible inspection.

## 5. NOMs (LFMN)

- Identify applicable NOMs in the Secretary of Economy search engine or with an accredited lab.
- Certify with an accredited lab; file with COFEPRIS / Energy / whoever applies.
- Keep reports and certificates at least 5 years (customs and audits request them).
- Practical budget USD 300-1,500 per certification. Run the gap analysis before launch.

## 6. Trademarks and patents (IMPI)

**Trademarks:** prior search -> Marca en Linea filing (~10% discount, ref. $2,425.66 + VAT) -> formal exam (1-2 mo) -> opposition (10 days) -> substantive exam (2-3 mo). Total 5-6 months unopposed. Declaration of use in year 3; renewal every 10 years.
**Patents:** Spanish specification + agent (mandatory for foreigners) -> filing (ref. $4,000 + $500 registration) -> publication at 18 mo -> exam up to 1 yr -> grant in 4-6 yrs (PPH 2-3). Annuities from year 5.

## 7. REPSE (STPS, only for specialized services/works)

1. Requirements: debt-free RFC + IMSS/INFONAVIT compliance + e.firma + deed + service description.
2. STPS portal filing; decision in ~20 business days. No government fee.
3. Valid 3 years (renewal only in the 3-month window before expiry).
4. Quarterly ICSOE (IMSS) and SISUB (INFONAVIT) reports; report changes within 20 days.

## 8. Founders' agreement (minimum contents)

- Authorized/subscribed/paid capital, distribution, preemptive rights, transfer restrictions.
- 4-year vesting + 12-month cliff (trust/options/buyback); phantom shares for employees.
- Drag-along (>=51%, usual range 50-75%) and tag-along.
- Non-compete 1-2 years, national/sectoral scope, with compensation (art. 5 Const.: must be reasonable).
- Disputes: mediation/arbitration first, then courts (e.g. CDMX); supermajorities; information rights.
- Sample clauses in `../research-matriz-contenidos.md` section 6.

| 3-4 | Public registry + pre-RFC + land-use check |
