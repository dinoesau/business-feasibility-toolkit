# Step 9 - Short legal path

> **This is not legal advice and it is valid for Mexico only.** This is a trimmed path to quote, verify, and schedule. The official details, fees, and timelines live in one place only: the [full legal framework guide](../../../guide/legal-framework-mx/). Always verify with the DOF, SAT, IMSS, IMPI, and your municipality, and confirm with an accountant or a lawyer before acting. The Spanish version is authoritative for legal terms.

Version en espanol: [Paso 9 - Ruta legal recortada](../../../guia/paso/paso-9-legal/).

## What you will achieve

Leave with a list of filings, their estimated costs, and an accountant appointment already scheduled. Nothing more: this step does not incorporate the company, it only tells you what the legal side costs and how long it takes so you can decide in step 10.

## What you need from the previous step

The closed numbers from step 8 (sensitivity). If the plan cannot survive the bad 20% case, do not spend on filings: adjust or stop here. Legal work cannot save a business with no margin.

## Step by step

1. **Tax regime (SAT).** Ask your accountant whether RESICO applies to your case; do not assume it. Verify at [sat.gob.mx](https://www.sat.gob.mx) and book your appointment at `citas.sat.gob.mx`. Details in section 2 (RFC and e.firma) of the [full guide](../../../guide/legal-framework-mx/).
2. **Trademark (IMPI / MARCANET).** Run the prior-art search on [MARCANET](https://marcanet.impi.gob.mx) before printing packaging, signage, or opening social accounts under that name. Details in section 6 (trademarks and patents) of the [full guide](../../../guide/legal-framework-mx/).
3. **Municipal license.** Check land use in your municipality's urban viewer or digital one-stop shop, and get a quote for the operating license for your activity. Details in section 4 (licenses and land use) of the [full guide](../../../guide/legal-framework-mx/).
4. **NOMs for your trade.** Identify the applicable ones in the Secretary of Economy search engine ([gob.mx/se](https://www.gob.mx/se)) or with an accredited lab, and run the gap analysis before launch. Details in section 5 (NOMs) of the [full guide](../../../guide/legal-framework-mx/).
5. **IMSS (if you will have payroll).** Get a quote for employer registration and worker filings on the [IMSS](https://www.imss.gob.mx) portal. Details in section 3 (IMSS and INFONAVIT) of the [full guide](../../../guide/legal-framework-mx/).
6. **Session with an accountant.** Bring your step 8 numbers and this list. Leave with a chosen regime, a tax calendar, and quoted filing costs. If you will provide specialized services, ask about REPSE (section 7 of the [full guide](../../../guide/legal-framework-mx/)); if there are partners, schedule the founders' agreement (section 8).

## Where to look for free

- Regime and appointments: [sat.gob.mx](https://www.sat.gob.mx) (`citas.sat.gob.mx` for the in-person appointment).
- Trademark: [MARCANET](https://marcanet.impi.gob.mx) and [gob.mx/impi](https://www.gob.mx/impi).
- License: your municipality's website and urban viewer.
- NOMs: Secretary of Economy search engine at [gob.mx/se](https://www.gob.mx/se).
- Payroll: [IMSS](https://www.imss.gob.mx) portal.
- Timeline and control table: sections 9 and 10 of the [full guide](../../../guide/legal-framework-mx/).

## Common mistakes

- Incorporating or leasing before closing the step 8 numbers.
- Printing packaging or signage without searching MARCANET.
- Running payroll without employer registration.
- Assuming RESICO applies without confirming it with the accountant and SAT.
- Copying fees and timelines from memory instead of verifying them at the official source.
- Keeping loose papers instead of a single file.

## Checklist

- [ ] Tax regime confirmed with the accountant (verified at sat.gob.mx).
- [ ] Trademark search run on MARCANET.
- [ ] Land use verified with the municipality and license quoted.
- [ ] Trade NOMs identified (gap analysis before launch).
- [ ] IMSS quoted if there will be payroll.
- [ ] Accountant session done, with regime, calendar, and costs in writing.
- [ ] Control table from the [full guide](../../../guide/legal-framework-mx/) copied to my file.
- [ ] Founders' agreement scheduled (only if there are partners).

> If you will seek investment, your next-level file is the corporate due diligence checklist (corporate, contracts, IP, tax, labor, and agreements): ask your lawyer or accountant for it. The starting base is the control table in the [full guide](../../../guide/legal-framework-mx/).
